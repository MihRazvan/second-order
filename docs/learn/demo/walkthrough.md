# Walkthrough

Open the utility. It boots armed on `REPLAY [ CROWD CAPTURE ON A +186% WALLET ]` with provenance **DEMO SCENARIO**.

1. Read the MAIN page. `SOURCE RETURN +186%` is the leaderboard number. `REMAINING ALPHA $13,925 OF $13,925` is the estimate before the test at your `FOLLOWER DELAY [ 5.0 SEC ]`.
2. Press **F5**. `CROWDGUARD VERDICT` spins `EVALUATING`.
3. Watch the recorder. Shadow-follower cells fill from the left, green first. `COMPETING FLOW` grows. `REMAINING ALPHA` drains and turns yellow.
4. Around the middle, cells past two seconds turn red and `EXEC DEPTH` shrinks. The status line reads *EXECUTION DETERIORATING*.
5. Then *SOURCE EXITS 55% · SOURCE-EXIT OVERLAP*. `REMAINING ALPHA` collapses to red.
6. At the end a Win95 dialog appears: **CrowdGuard — crowd capture risk. RESIZE — do not copy at $1,000. Scenario-adjusted outcome −12.4%. Maximum scenario-compatible size $84.** Buttons: Resize to $84, Show evidence, Share report, Block wallet, Copy anyway (disabled), OK.
7. Click **Resize to $84**. `INTENDED SIZE` becomes `[ $84 ]` and the verdict reads `** ALLOW **`.
8. Press **↓** to `FOLLOWER DELAY`, then **−** a few times. The capacity map and the verdict re-solve instantly, in the browser.
9. Press **F9**. The Evidence Log shows provenance, the verdict breakdown, data quality, model inputs, the latest quote column, the security snapshot, the competing-flow ledger and the assumptions.
10. Press **F10**. The report page opens with your size and delay in the URL fragment. Copy link, print, or send.

Reset with **ESC ESC**.
